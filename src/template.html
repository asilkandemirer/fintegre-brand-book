    01 · What is Fintegre

    Fintegre is the trade-in and payment infrastructure behind device renewal.

    We value the device a customer already has, turn it into credit in seconds and help the store hand over the new one.

    1. {{icon:value}}01 · VALUE

      A clear price in seconds.

      Phones, tablets, computers and watches each have their own valuation flow.

    2. {{icon:pay}}02 · PAY

      Value becomes credit on the spot.

      Payment, operations and settlement run in the background.

    3. {{icon:upgrade}}03 · RENEW

      The new device goes home.

      The customer upgrades with confidence; the store closes a better sale.

    04 · Logo

    Four blocks. One slant: 65°.

    The mark is built from four parallelograms. Every edge leans at the same angle, measured from the master artwork and reused across the whole system.

    Behind every device, a number.

    Fintegre reads a device as data: model, condition, market. The mark carries that idea in its own surface.
