02 — Colour

Two primaries.

Five tones.

Endless blends.

Midnight Graphite is the ground. Deep Indigo is the signal. Every other tone is derived from these two.

Indigo lightens into Ice Blue. Graphite lifts into Soft Titanium. Where the two families meet: White Smoke.

Every gradient runs between two tones of the palette. The room behind the mark is always a blend, never a new colour.
