01 · What is Fintegre

Fintegre is the trade-in and payment infrastructure behind device renewal.

We value the device a customer already has, turn it into credit in seconds and help the store hand over the new one. Retailers, telecom operators and device brands run their renewal programs on Fintegre.

  1. {{icon:value}}01 · Value

    A clear price in seconds.

    Phones, tablets, computers and watches each have their own valuation flow.

  2. {{icon:pay}}02 · Pay

    Value becomes credit on the spot.

    Payment, operations and settlement run in the background, in one flow.

  3. {{icon:upgrade}}03 · Renew

    The new device goes home.

    The customer upgrades with confidence and the store closes a better sale.

What we are

  • B2B
  • White-label
  • Vendor-neutral
  • Asset-light

What we are not

  • A consumer trade-in site
  • A marketplace for used devices
  • A device buyer
  • A recycling brand

07 · Graphic language

One angle runs through everything.

The 65° slant comes from the mark itself. Repeated in lines, blocks, crops and light, it makes a Fintegre surface recognisable before the logo appears.

Why 65°?

  1. It is ours.The angle is measured from the mark. Nobody else owns it.
  2. It moves forward.A 65° line rises like an upgrade arrow and reads as progress.
  3. It stays calm.Steep enough to feel alive, never sharp enough to feel aggressive.

Lines

1 px lines at 65°, 12 px apart. A texture for Ice and Indigo fields.

Blocks

Colour fields meet along a 65° edge, never at a random angle.

Crops

One image corner can be cut at 65°. The other three stay square.

Light and motion

Light falls along the slant and elements enter along it too.

Rule

Use one 65° element per layout. Two at most, and they never cross.

11 · Photography

Real people in soft, white light.

Our photography shows the moment a device makes life easier. A diverse cast, gallery-simple places and daylight, always with a premium device in use.

Cast

Women and men aged 25 to 65, from many backgrounds. Natural expressions, real skin.

Light

Soft daylight through large windows. High-key, never washed out, shadows at 65°.

Place

Gallery-white rooms, pale stone and light oak, with plenty of empty wall.

Device

Premium silver devices in use. Screens show soft light only, never third-party logos.

Four shot types

Every image set mixes all four, so a feed or a page never repeats itself.

Lifestyle

  • Genuine moments of relief and joy
  • A premium device in every frame

Avoid

  • Dark, moody grading or neon light

12 · Tone of voice

Say what it changes.

Fintegre sounds like a calm expert at the counter. Clear first, warm always and never louder than the moment.

01

Clear first

Lead with the outcome. One idea per sentence.

02

Show the number

Values, times and steps say more than adjectives.

03

Warm, never cute

Friendly and respectful. No jokes about money.

04

Short and active

Verbs up front. Cut every word that does no work.

We say“Your iPad's trade-in value is ready.”

We don't say“Exciting news! Unlock amazing value today!”

We say“Trade-in programs that raise device value and lift conversion.”

We don't say“Revolutionary AI-powered synergy for recommerce.”

We say“Bring your old phone. See its value in a minute.”

We don't say“Fintegre is delighted to announce a disruptive solution.”

13 · Instagram

A feed that draws the slant.

Every post follows five rules. Together they turn the profile grid into one calm picture, with the colour posts falling along a diagonal.

  • LogoThe mark only, bottom left, at 8% of the post width. White on photos and Indigo, Indigo on Ice.
  • AngleOne 65° element per post: a line, a block edge or a crop.
  • ColourPhoto posts stay light. Colour posts are Indigo or Ice, full bleed.
  • TypeWhite Geologica on photos over a soft Night Indigo veil. Eight words at most.
  • RhythmTwo photos, then one colour post. On the grid the colour posts form a diagonal.
