# Fintegre Brand Book — Image Prompt Pack (GPT Image)

24 images in 5 batches. Paste **one batch per message**: first the STYLE block, then the batch's shot list. Save every image with the exact file name shown (e.g. `A1-unboxing.jpg`) and send them all back in one message. I place each file by its name.

**Attachments:** Batch D (merchandise) needs the logo as a reference image. Attach the files from the `logo-references` folder:
- Light surfaces: `fintegre-mark-gradient.png` or `fintegre-lockup-dark.png`
- Dark or indigo surfaces: `fintegre-mark-white.png` or `fintegre-lockup-white.png`

**Devices:** Prompts ask for premium aluminium devices with no visible logos. This keeps the brand book free of third-party trademarks. If Fintegre uses official Apple product photography, it can replace these images later.

---

## STYLE (paste at the start of every batch)

```
Art direction for all images in this message:
Premium editorial lifestyle photography for a European technology brand called Fintegre.
Mood: calm, optimistic, quietly confident — the feeling of an Apple keynote film or an OpenAI brand campaign, never a stock photo.
Light: soft white daylight, large diffused windows, gentle shadows, high-key but not washed out.
Places: gallery-white interiors, pale stone, light oak, brushed aluminium, plenty of empty wall. Art-gallery simplicity.
People: a diverse cast across the set — Black, East Asian, South Asian, Middle Eastern, Latina/Latino and white European women and men, ages 25–65. Natural, unposed expressions, real skin texture, simple premium clothing in white, stone, grey, navy, with an occasional deep indigo (#3047EC) or ice blue (#B7D7FF) piece.
Colour: neutral world with small accents of deep indigo #3047EC and ice blue #B7D7FF only. No neon, no purple-black "hacker" mood, no dark moody grading.
Devices: modern premium smartphone, laptop and tablet in silver aluminium with NO visible brand logos. Screens show only soft blurred light or a simple white interface, no readable text.
Composition: clean, one clear subject, generous negative space so headlines can sit on the image. When it fits naturally, let one strong line, shadow or edge run diagonally at about 65 degrees.
Camera: full-frame, 35mm or 50mm, f/2–f/2.8, shallow depth of field, photographic realism, no illustration, no CGI look.
Never include: third-party logos, readable UI text, watermarks, extra fingers, distorted hands.
```
